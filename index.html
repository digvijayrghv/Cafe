Our Story

A Café Built Around Slowing Down

Brew & Bean started as a small corner counter with a simple idea: good coffee shouldn't be rushed. Years later, we're still roasting with care, baking daily, and keeping the doors open for anyone who needs a warm seat and a good cup.
